# SAP Technology Stack

## What is SAP?

SAP (Systems, Applications, and Products in Data Processing) is one of the world's leading Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) platforms used to manage business operations, finance, supply chains, human resources, procurement, manufacturing, and customer relationships.
